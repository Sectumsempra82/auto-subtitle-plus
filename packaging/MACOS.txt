Auto Subtitle Plus v0.3.0-rc.4 — Apple Silicon macOS preview

If your download is a .pkg, open it and follow Installer to install or replace
Auto Subtitle Plus in /Applications. Finish running jobs and quit the app first.
Administrator authorization may be required. Do not uninstall the old app first.
The installer replaces the whole app bundle; settings, queue and models stay
outside it. It does not add an automatic updater or download dependencies.

For the ZIP edition, extract it, drag Auto Subtitle Plus.app into Applications
and choose Replace when Finder asks. Avoid Keep Both to prevent duplicate apps.

Requires macOS 26 or newer on Apple Silicon and FFmpeg/ffprobe on PATH, /opt/homebrew/bin or
/usr/local/bin. For Homebrew users: brew install ffmpeg
Python, Qt and processing libraries are bundled; model weights are not.
Internet is needed to acquire models. Cached models support offline processing.
The Mac GUI offers local processing and local translation only. Start with CPU;
CUDA and Hy-MT2 are not supported by this Mac preview.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized. macOS may block
the installer or the app’s first launch. The installer is also unsigned. Only if you trust this download, use System Settings > Privacy
& Security > Open Anyway after attempting to open it. Do not disable Gatekeeper.
Validated on an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 27.0; older supported versions
and Intel Macs have not been validated. This is a release candidate.

Settings/queue: ~/Library/Application Support/AutoSubtitlePlus/desktop/state.json
Model caches normally use ~/.cache; configured cache paths remain supported.
Your media, settings and models are not included in the distributed package.
